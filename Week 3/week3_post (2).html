How we tested the network

We work on the largest connected component of an undirected version of the Marvel graph. This keeps the question focused on the part of the network in which every character can reach every other character.

Centrality is not dependency

Betweenness measures how often a character sits on shortest routes. Removal damage asks a different question: does the rest of the network still remain connected without that character?

The two measures should be related, but they do not have to select the same character. Dense alternative routes can make a highly central node surprisingly replaceable.
